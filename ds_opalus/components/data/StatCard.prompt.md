KPI tile for dashboards and slide metric rows. Figures are Barlow tabular so columns align.

```jsx
<StatCard label="Ocupação" value="92,4" unit="%" delta="+4,2 p.p." icon="bed" footnote="Últimas 24h" />
<StatCard tone="brand" label="Leitos ativos" value="1.284" />
```

Use at most one `tone="brand"` tile per row.
